# 📊 Sales Revenue Analysis – Case Study
🧠 Business Problem

A company wants to analyze its sales performance across different products and categories in order to:

Identify best-selling products

Understand revenue distribution

Discover market and sales trends

Support better business and inventory decisions

The analysis is performed using real-world business scenarios and focuses on answering key questions that a data analyst would typically solve.

# 🎯 Objective of the Case Study

The main objective of this case study is to perform an end-to-end sales analysis using Excel, SQL, and Power BI, and transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

📌 Problem Statements & Tasks
🔹 Task 1: Data Preparation & Cleaning (Excel)

Certain columns in Product_Sales and Product_Pricing datasets contained missing values.

These missing values were identified and updated using data available in other sheets.

This ensured data consistency and accuracy for further analysis.

🔹 Task 2: Sales Contribution Analysis (Excel)

A new column Sales Contribution (%) was created.

This column calculates how much each product contributes to the total sales revenue.

This helps in identifying high-impact products that drive business revenue.

🔹 Task 3: Category-Wise Performance Analysis (Excel)

Total Sales and Average Order Value (AOV) were calculated for each product category.

Categories were sorted in descending order of total sales to highlight top-performing segments.

AOV was calculated using:

AOV = Total Sales / Total Number of Unique Transactions

🔹 Task 4: Product Demand Analysis (Excel)

The most purchased product was identified based on total quantity sold.

In case of a tie, all top products were listed.

This analysis helps understand customer demand patterns.

🔹 Task 5: Pivot Analysis & Visualization (Excel)

A Pivot Table was created to compare total sales across product categories.

A Pivot Chart was generated to visually represent category-wise sales performance.

The chart includes proper titles and alignment for clear interpretation.

# 🛢 SQL-Based Business Insights

SQL queries were written to answer key business questions, including:

Total revenue generated by each product category

Product with the highest discount percentage

Category with the highest average price

Product with the lowest price fluctuation

Category contributing the highest total revenue

These queries demonstrate the use of joins, aggregations, sorting, and calculations.

# 📈 Power BI – Dynamic Dashboard

A fully interactive Power BI dashboard was created to visualize sales insights dynamically.

Dashboard Features:

Key KPIs:
Total Transactions

Total Discounts Applied

Percentage of Sales by Product Type

Visualizations:

Clustered Column Chart (Sales by Category)

Pie Chart (Product Segment Distribution)

Line Chart (Sales Trends)

Interactive filters and slicers for:

Product Type

Discount Percentage

This dashboard enables quick decision-making through visual storytelling.

🚀 Outcome

This case study demonstrates the ability to:

Clean and prepare raw data

Perform analytical calculations

Write optimized SQL queries

Build interactive dashboards

Translate data into actionable business insights
